## Setup

1. Start eswm on a TTY in tiling mode. Confirm the session is live (pointer
   moves, no errors in `~/.local/state/eswm/eswm.log`).
2. Open three tiled windows. The default Master split must look unchanged:
   master is 60% of inner width; the two stack windows are equal height.
3. Note the starting rects visually or from the log's layout trace.

## Master layout

1. **Master/stack boundary** — hover within 6 px of the vertical boundary and
   drag right ~100 px. Master widens, stack narrows; cursor is `ew-resize`.
2. **15% floor, right** — drag the same boundary far right. Master stops at
   85% of inner width; the stack column stays at least 15% and usable.
3. **15% floor, left** — drag far left. Master stops at 15%; no zero-width
   column, no flicker, no `u32` wrap.
4. **Stack boundary** — drag the horizontal boundary between the two stack
   windows ~50 px. Upper window grows, lower shrinks; their **widths do not
   change**; cursor is `ns-resize`.
5. **Stack floor** — drag the stack boundary to either extreme. The smaller
   window keeps at least 15% of the stack height.
6. **Outer frame** — press near the output edge or a window centre. No split
   drag starts; click-to-focus behaves normally.

## Equal layout

7. Switch with `es.dsp.set_tiling_layout("equal")` or the configured keybind.
   Open four windows so a 2×2 grid is chosen.
8. **Column boundary** — drag an internal vertical boundary. Only that row's
   adjacent column widths change; rows stay aligned; cursor is `ew-resize`.
9. **Row boundary** — drag an internal horizontal boundary. Only that column's
   adjacent row heights change; columns stay aligned; cursor is `ns-resize`.
10. **Grid floor** — drag both boundaries to extremes; no cell collapses.

## Persistence across rearrangements

11. Set a non-default Master ratio and stack split. Open a fourth window:
    earlier ratios are preserved.
12. Switch workspace away and back: ratios are preserved for the session.
13. Resize the output (or plug a second monitor): ratios still produce valid,
    non-overlapping cells; no titlebar overlap on lower stack rows.
14. Multi-output sanity: splits are per output; dragging on one monitor does
    not change another monitor's layout.

## Reset and input semantics

15. Invoke `es.dsp.reset_tiling_splits()` from config or a keybind bound to
    `reset_tiling_splits`. Master returns to 60%, grid cells become equal.
16. **Click without travel** — press on a boundary and release without moving.
    No split change.
17. **Escape cancel** — start a split drag, move ~50 px, press Escape. The
    split stops changing; the held button is still swallowed on release.
18. **Titlebar buttons** — with titlebar enabled, buttons still win over a

19. **Client sizing** — after a split drag, clients reconfigure to the new
